Futures soften into Mag 7 + FOMC apex — oil rips, gold sells, dispersion is the trade.

US equity tape opens Tuesday with a modest fade after Monday's firm close. Pre-tape quotes show SPY at 712.54 (−0.37%), QQQ 659.36 (−0.73%), IWM 275.80 (−0.49%), DIA 492.65 (+0.17%) — a defensive lean concentrated in tech, with the Dow holding bid[1]. The Apr 28 calendar is real but secondary to the event week: Case-Shiller and FHFA at 9:00 ET, Consumer Confidence and Richmond Fed Manufacturing at 10:00 ET[3]. The market is pricing the Wednesday gauntlet, not today's data.

Two macro tells dominate the open. USO is +3.03% to 138.80 — a clean second-leg of the Strait-of-Hormuz bid that started Monday[1][2]. GLD is −2.02% to 421.20 the same morning, an unusual pair: oil-as-tail-hedge bid while gold-as-tail-hedge unwinds. VIXY +1.65% rounds out the defensive posture; UUP +0.24% says no dollar break. TLT −0.12% is a quiet tape into FOMC, but duration has not turned.

The week's center of gravity is Wednesday after-hours — MSFT, META, GOOGL, AMZN, QCOM all print on the same evening[4]. Today's tape includes KO, GM, UPS, SPOT, SBUX, BKNG, V, TMUS, NXPI, QCOM among the front line[4]. SPOT already printed −13.91% pre-open as one of the day's largest losers, with RMBS −21.14% and ARE −12.23% leading the downside cohort. KFRC +36.27% and OMCL +20.33% top the gainer board — single-name dispersion is wide before bell.

Regime read: BULLISH by the run model, but the trade is dispersion, not direction. SPY is a fade while individual names live and die on prints. Carry oil as a hedge against the equity book; do not chase gold lower; let SPOT and RMBS settle before any post-print engagement. The bar Wednesday is guidance, not headlines, and Tuesday's job is to be sized for it.
