Evening Playbook · Apr 27 2026 · Session #019

Tape holds firm into Mag 7 + Fed week — Hormuz oil bid, AI leadership bifurcates.

US stocks edged higher Monday as investors digested a reported Iran proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz[1]. SPY printed 715.10 (+0.16%), QQQ 664.24 (+0.05%), IWM 277.06 (+0.15%), DIA 491.83 (−0.08%) — a flat-to-firm session at the open of a heavyweight event week. S&P futures had been little changed around 7:40 ET[3]; intraday a +2% WTI move on Hormuz tensions briefly pressured tech[4], but the index kept its bid into the close.

Leadership bifurcated. NVDA ripped +4.00% to 216.61 and MU added +5.60% to 524.56 — AI-infra and memory regained the wheel[8]. Cybersecurity caught a real bid: PANW +2.44%, CRWD +1.45%, with software ETF flows doing the work[4]. The other side of semis was ugly: AMD −3.83%, ARM −8.06%, MRVL −3.71%, LRCX −3.07% — pockets of profit-taking after the April run, while QCOM +0.95% caught a bid on OpenAI/MediaTek smartphone-processor reporting[4].

The week is the test. Five Magnificent 7 names report and the Fed meets[2][7]. Tuesday brings KMB, GM, UPS, V, SBUX; Wednesday is the apex with MSFT, META, GOOGL, AMZN, QCOM[2]; AAPL closes the marquee later in the week[2][5]. VZ and DPZ already reported Monday[2]. With S&P up >9% and Nasdaq >15% in April[1], the bar is high — guidance, not prints, will price this tape.

Macro tail: USO +1.75% to 134.72 as the Hormuz/Iran path stays unresolved[6][7]. TLT −0.50% to 86.28 — duration weak into the Fed. The regime is risk-on but event-heavy: oil is the live tail-hedge, breadth is selective, and AAPL (−1.27%) is the megacap on the wrong side of trend going into its own print.
