Midday: small-caps lead the fade as oil pushes through $100 — event risk still points to Wed AH.

At midday, risk is leaning defensive: SPY 711.61 (−0.50%), QQQ 658.04 (−0.93%), and IWM 273.48 (−1.32%). Small-caps are underperforming, a classic “breadth leak” signal when the tape is trying to hold highs.[1]

The tell on the day remains energy: WTI is 99.91 (+3.67%) with a $100 handle in play, while 10Y is 4.36% (+0.42%). Higher energy + firm rates is a tougher cocktail for duration and cyclicals into the close.[1][4]

Single-name dispersion is still extreme. On the upside: KFRC (+40.52%), KNSA (+23.24%), OMCL (+20.36%). On the downside: ERAS (−48.98%), RMBS (−21.39%), NVTS (−17.95%), CLS (−15.71%). This remains an “idiosyncratic tape” — trade levels, not narratives.[2][3]

Timing map: the market is still positioned around Wednesday after-hours megacap prints (MSFT/META/GOOGL/AMZN) rather than today’s intraday flow. Into the afternoon, watch for rate/energy follow-through to decide whether the tape grinds lower or snaps back.
