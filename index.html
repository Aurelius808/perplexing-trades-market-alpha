Setup Brief · Super Wednesday · Apr 29 2026 · Session #022

The tape is not quiet — Apr 28 risk-off into a stacked Fed + Mag 4 print day.

This is a manual setup update, not a scheduled session. Tuesday's cash close came in lower from records: the Nasdaq Composite gave back roughly 1.3%, the S&P 500 retreated near 0.7%, and the Dow finished close to flat. Selling concentrated in AI / semiconductor names as OpenAI-related doubts pressured the AI complex — a reminder that leadership and risk-on are not the same thing into a binary event window.[1]

Wednesday is the gating day. The FOMC decision lands at 14:00 ET, with markets pricing a hold at 3.50%–3.75%; a dovish statement could support rate-sensitive equities while a hawkish tone tightens financial conditions, the dollar, and the long end.[2] Then after the close, four hyperscalers — Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft — print on the same evening. Together they represent more than 19% of S&P 500 weight and carry a combined 2026 capex budget near $650B; the print is a referendum on AI ROI as much as a quarter.[3]

The capex tape underneath those names keeps moving higher. 2026 hyperscaler AI capex consensus has climbed by roughly $250B versus 2025, from about $379B to $622B — investors are paying for the buildout, but the patience window is finite.[4] Watchpoints: Microsoft on Azure / OpenAI commercialization and the capex path; Meta on ads, Family of Apps margin, Reality Labs losses, and AI / data-center spend.[4]

Macro overlay: the Conference Board consumer confidence read printed 92.8, a 2026 high, which softens but does not remove the event risk; the Hormuz / oil channel remains an inflation pressure if the bid persists.[1] Posture into Wednesday: selective de-risking before binary catalysts, hedge into the prints, react after.
